APEX LOGISTICS - CONFLICTING CASE 10: DATA SENSITIVITY CLASSIFICATION
Document ID: DOC-CONF-010
Topic: Compensation & Salary Transparency Classification

CONFLICT DETAILS:
Section A (Data Transparency Policy DOC-GOV-02): "Employee compensation bands and pay structures are classified as Level 1 Public Internal and readable by all staff."
Section B (Data Security Policy DOC-SEC-01): "Payroll data and salary structures are strictly Level 4 Restricted Confidential; unauthorized viewing is a disciplinary violation."
CONTRADICTION: Compensation data is classified as Public Internal in Data Transparency vs Restricted Confidential in Data Security.
