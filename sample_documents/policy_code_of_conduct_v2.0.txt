APEX LOGISTICS - CODE OF CONDUCT
Document ID: DOC-POL-009
Version: 2.0 (Effective 2024-11-01, CURRENT ACTIVE VERSION)

1. Vendor Gifts
Strict Zero-Gift Policy: Employees are prohibited from accepting any gifts, gratuities, or hospitality of any value from suppliers or vendors.

2. Social Media Guidelines
Workplace photos inside facilities or near customer manifest data are strictly forbidden to prevent data exposure.
