APEX LOGISTICS - DATA PRIVACY POLICY
Document ID: DOC-POL-007
Version: 2.0 (Effective 2024-05-01, CURRENT ACTIVE VERSION)

1. Customer Data Retention
Customer PII must be purged/anonymized after 2 years unless active litigation exists (GDPR & CCPA compliance).

2. Data Access
Customer PII access is restricted strictly on a Need-to-Know basis via role-based access control (RBAC).
