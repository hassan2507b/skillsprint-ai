APEX LOGISTICS - HEALTH & SAFETY POLICY
Document ID: DOC-POL-005
Version: 1.0 (Effective 2020-01-01, DEPRECATED)

1. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
Warehouse workers must wear hard hats. Steel-toe safety shoes are recommended but optional.

2. Incident Reporting
Injuries must be reported to safety officers within 48 hours.
