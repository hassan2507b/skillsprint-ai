APEX LOGISTICS - HEALTH & SAFETY POLICY
Document ID: DOC-POL-005
Version: 2.0 (Effective 2024-01-01, CURRENT ACTIVE VERSION)

1. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
Steel-toe boots, high-visibility reflective vests, and hard hats are strictly MANDATORY for all warehouse supervisors, staff, and delivery drivers entering load zones.

2. Incident Reporting
All safety incidents and near-misses must be reported within 2 hours via the Safety App.
