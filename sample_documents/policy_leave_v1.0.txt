APEX LOGISTICS - LEAVE POLICY
Document ID: DOC-POL-001
Version: 1.0 (Effective 2022-01-01, DEPRECATED)
Status: Superceded by Version 2.0

1. Annual Leave Entitlement
Employees are granted 15 paid annual leave days per calendar year.

2. Sick Leave Notification
Employees must notify their manager at least 2 hours prior to the shift start.

3. Maternity & Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 12 weeks. Paternity leave is 1 week paid.
