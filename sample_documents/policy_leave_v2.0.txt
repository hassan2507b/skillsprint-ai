APEX LOGISTICS - LEAVE POLICY
Document ID: DOC-POL-001
Version: 2.0 (Effective 2025-01-01, CURRENT ACTIVE VERSION)
Status: Replaces Version 1.0

1. Annual Leave Entitlement
Employees are granted 22 paid annual leave days per calendar year. (Updated from 15 days in v1.0).

2. Sick Leave Notification
Employees must notify their manager at least 1 hour prior to shift start via HR portal. (Updated from 2 hours in v1.0).

3. Maternity & Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is extended to 16 weeks paid. Paternity leave is extended to 4 weeks paid.
