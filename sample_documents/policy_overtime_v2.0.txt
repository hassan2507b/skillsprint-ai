APEX LOGISTICS - OVERTIME WORK POLICY
Document ID: DOC-POL-006
Version: 2.0 (Effective 2025-02-01, CURRENT ACTIVE VERSION)

1. Approval Process
Overtime work MUST be pre-approved in writing by the Department Manager at least 24 hours in advance in the payroll system.

2. Maximum Overtime Hours
Overtime is strictly capped at 12 hours per week per employee to comply with labor regulations.
