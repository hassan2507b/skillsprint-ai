APEX LOGISTICS - REMOTE WORK POLICY
Document ID: DOC-POL-003
Version: 1.0 (Effective 2022-03-01, DEPRECATED)
Status: Superceded by Version 2.0

1. Work Location Flexibility
Employees in eligible office roles may work 100% remotely from any location within the country.

2. Home Office Stipend
Employees receive a one-time setup reimbursement of $1,000 for home office furniture.
