APEX LOGISTICS - REMOTE WORK POLICY
Document ID: DOC-POL-003
Version: 2.0 (Effective 2025-01-01, CURRENT ACTIVE VERSION)
Status: Replaces Version 1.0

1. Work Location Flexibility
Employees in office roles operate under a Hybrid model: minimum 3 mandatory days in-office per week (Tuesday to Thursday), max 2 days remote.

2. Home Office Stipend
Home office reimbursement is capped at $400 for ergonomic equipment with prior IT & HR approval.
