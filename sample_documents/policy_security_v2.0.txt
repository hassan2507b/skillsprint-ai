APEX LOGISTICS - SECURITY POLICY
Document ID: DOC-POL-002
Version: 2.0 (Effective 2024-09-01, CURRENT ACTIVE VERSION)
Status: Replaces Version 1.0

1. Password Standards
All employee account passwords must be at least 16 characters long. Periodic mandatory resets are eliminated in favor of passphrase complexity and leak monitoring.

2. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
MFA is strictly mandatory for ALL logins (both internal and remote) using hardware tokens or authenticator apps.

3. USB Storage Media
Removable USB drives are strictly blocked and prohibited on all corporate laptops and endpoints.
